A local place to see what matters.

Life OS Personal is a private, personal-use dashboard that brings selected context, commitments and planning into one place.

Its Google connection helps the account owner review recent email and upcoming calendar events alongside their own notes. It supports planning, prioritization and remembering follow-ups.

Collect

Read-only collectors check selected sources approximately every ten minutes while the computer and worker are running.

Review

The owner can review collected context in a local dashboard and in user-authorized AI check-ins.

Decide

Suggestions help surface possible priorities. The Google connection has no permission to send email or change events.

How the Google connection works

The owner authorizes a desktop OAuth client. Renewable credentials are saved in macOS Keychain. The collectors read Google data and save observations in the owner’s project folder; regular collection itself makes no AI model calls.

The current email collector reads recent message metadata and snippets, with exclusions for certain categories and detected authentication material. It does not fetch message bodies or attachments. The calendar collector reads a rolling window of primary-calendar events. The granted Google permissions allow broader read access than these current collection limits.

This public site is app information only. It does not host the private dashboard, display account data, collect credentials or provide a sign-in form. The dashboard runs locally.

Data and control

The project folder is in an iCloud-synced location, and selected context may be made available to an AI provider during user-authorized reviews. This is not a promise that data stays exclusively on one device.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, use and deletion details, and the usage notice for the prototype’s limits.
