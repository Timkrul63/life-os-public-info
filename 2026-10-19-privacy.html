Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Applies to Life OS Personal and its personal-use Google connection.

Life OS Personal is a local personal-information and planning prototype operated by its account owner. This policy describes its present Google integration and the handling of information collected through it.

Google data accessed

The connection requests these two OAuth permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — read-only Gmail access.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly — read-only calendar event access.

These permissions permit wider reading than the current collector performs. They do not authorize sending or changing email, or creating, changing or deleting calendar events.

The current Gmail collector saves message identifiers, subject lines, message times, thread references, incoming/outgoing indicators, snippets and source links. Its first collection covers two recent days; subsequent checks use a six-hour overlap from the last successful window. It excludes Spam, Trash, Promotions and Social categories and messages detected as containing authentication material. Those filters reduce collection; they are not a guarantee that every sensitive message is detected. It does not fetch message bodies or attachments.

The current calendar collector saves primary-calendar event identifiers, titles, start/end and update times, status and source links, including cancellations. Its rolling window runs from one day before collection through fourteen days after it. An event’s presence does not establish attendance.

Purpose and use

Collected information supports the owner’s personal dashboard, briefings, planning, follow-up detection and user-directed assistance. Deterministic collectors normally check every ten minutes while the computer and worker are available. They do not call AI models.

User-authorized check-ins and other AI reviews may make relevant collected context available to the AI provider used for that review. The owner controls the chosen tools and review workflow. Life OS does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage, processing and sharing

Collected observations, source references, review records and derived notes are saved in the owner’s local project folder. That folder is in an iCloud-synced location, so copies may also be processed or stored through the owner’s iCloud account and synced devices. Google authorization credentials are stored separately in macOS Keychain, rather than project records.

Google processes API requests. The hosting provider serves these public information pages. Apple may process synced project files. When the owner uses an AI service to review context, that provider may receive the selected information; its handling is also subject to the chosen service’s terms and settings. These are distinct from the regular local collectors. This policy does not assert that all information remains on one device or that another provider retains no data.

The public information site contains no private dashboard data, sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising or tracking embeds. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own policies.

Google API Limited Use

Life OS Personal’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide and improve the owner’s personal-facing features described here. Transfers are limited to what is necessary for those features with the owner’s authorization, or as required for security or applicable law. It is not transferred for advertising, sale, or generalized AI model training.

Security and limits

The Google integration uses HTTPS with certificate and hostname verification. Renewable credentials are held in the named macOS Keychain connection. The private dashboard is served on the local computer. Project files and synced copies rely on the owner’s device, account and storage protections; observations are not separately encrypted by this prototype. No system can guarantee complete security.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Project observations and derived records remain until the owner removes them. There is currently no automatic retention deadline or cleanup policy. The owner can stop the collector and revoke Life OS Personal’s access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized Google collection; it does not delete information already saved.

To remove saved information, the owner must delete the relevant local project records, derived notes and any synced copies or backups they control, and separately remove the named Keychain connection if desired. Information already provided to an AI service may require a separate deletion request or action in that service. Removing a local record does not guarantee deletion from backups or another provider’s systems.

Questions and changes

This is a personal-use app, not a public account service. Questions or deletion requests should be directed to the operator who configured the local Life OS instance. Material changes to data access or handling should be reflected in this policy before the changed workflow is used. The effective date identifies this version.
