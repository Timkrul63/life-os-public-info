Usage notice

Effective October 19, 2026

Life OS Personal is a personal-use prototype for organizing context and supporting the account owner’s planning. It is not currently offered as a public commercial service.

Use and authority

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to use. Google’s consent screen controls the permissions you grant. The current Google integration is read-only: it cannot send email or create, edit or delete calendar events.

Review before relying on it

Collection may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Filters and rolling collection windows can omit information. Summaries and AI-generated suggestions can contain errors. The owner should check original sources and make their own decisions, especially for deadlines or consequential matters.

The prototype does not guarantee continuous operation, complete records, successful reminders or particular outcomes. Its planning assistance is not a substitute for qualified professional advice.

Data handling and ending use

The privacy policy describes the Google data collected, local and synced storage, user-authorized AI reviews and deletion limits. You can stop the local worker and revoke the app through Google Account connections. Revocation does not remove data previously saved.

Public pages and private workspace

These public pages explain the app. They do not provide access to its private local dashboard, account information or credentials. The account owner remains responsible for their device, account permissions and chosen review tools.

Changes

The prototype may evolve. Changes to permissions or data handling should be reviewed by the owner and reflected in the relevant app information before use. This notice describes the current personal prototype and makes no product or support guarantees.
